Information used for Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google,” Google shares basic account information such as your name, email address, and account identifier with this site through Supabase Authentication. We use it to sign you in and show which account is signed in. This site does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other Google services.

Study information and leaderboard

Study progress, preferences, and timer sessions are saved in your browser on your device. If you sign in with Google, saved Study Timer hours and your Google display name are also stored in the site's Supabase database so signed-in students can see the live leaderboard. The leaderboard shows your display name and total saved study hours to other signed-in users. Clearing browser data may remove local study information, but does not remove leaderboard data already stored in Supabase.

Service providers

Google provides the sign-in service, and Supabase provides authentication and database services. They process information as needed to operate these services and under their own privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy and Supabase Privacy Policy.

Use and sharing

Account information is used to provide sign-in, basic account display, and leaderboard ranking. We do not sell personal information or use Google account data for advertising. This site does not currently take payments.

Account and local data

You can sign out from the site. To request deletion of your sign-in account and associated leaderboard entry, contact shreyashpatel0308@gmail.com. To remove study data saved on your device, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
